# Changelog

Every change made to this template after it was first published, most recent first.
You forked once; this is how you know what has moved since.

To pick up a change:

```bash
git pull upstream main
```

(The `upstream` remote is set up in step 7 of `INSTRUCTIONS.md` §1.)

Changes are also announced in class. If a change affects what is being asked of you
rather than how, it will be announced before it is published.

## Unreleased

- **No mandatory questions.** Every research question is yours; section 6 describes the
  dataset and the *shape* a good question takes, and nothing more. `docs/QUESTIONS.md`
  lost its "mandatory" section.
- **No pre-registration tag.** Questions are still written before the results, and
  changes are still logged with their reason, but nothing is registered and the
  `part1-prereg` tag is gone.
- **Three ways to hand in**: a public fork with the link, a private fork with
  `antoninofurnari` added as collaborator, or a zip by e-mail. Tagging is recommended,
  not required.
- **Groups are registered with the course form (MS Forms)** in the first two weeks;
  datasets go out by e-mail in the third.
- **Part 1 is reviewed in class** at the session of the first written test, and does not
  have to be finished by then.
- First publication of the template for A.Y. 2026/27.
